03

Ports of call

Two very different Bahamian stops — a curated private-island day and the real, historic capital.

Perfect Day at CocoCay
Royal Caribbean's private island · Fri, Jan 1
  • Thrill WaterparkHome to Daredevil's Peak, the tallest waterslide in North America, plus a wave pool and dozens of slides (separate ticket).
  • Oasis LagoonThe largest freshwater pool in the Caribbean, with a swim-up bar — included.
  • Up, Up & AwayA helium balloon ride to ~450 feet for island-and-sea views (separate ticket).
  • Beaches & clubsChill Island and South Beach are included; Coco Beach Club and Overwater Cabanas are the premium add-ons.
Worth knowing
  • Lunch and the beaches/pools are included; the waterpark, cabanas, and balloon are extra — and they sell out, so book before you sail if you want them.
  • It's a dock-and-walk island (no tender), so you can come and go from the ship all day — easy to grab a quiet morning beach before the crowds.
Nassau
New Providence, Bahamas · Sun, Jan 3
  • Paradise Island & AtlantisAcross the bridge — the famous resort, its aquarium and water park (day passes available), and Royal Caribbean's new Royal Beach Club.
  • Queen's Staircase & Fort Fincastle66 steps carved from limestone leading up to an 18th-century fort with harbor views.
  • Junkanoo & Cable BeachLively town beach steps from the port, or the longer stretch of Cable Beach a short ride west.
  • Straw Market & downtownBahamian crafts, conch fritters, and colonial architecture within walking distance of the pier.
Worth adding
  • Popular excursions book up fast over the holidays — swim-with-pigs day trips, Blue Lagoon, and Atlantis Aquaventure are the classics.
  • For a low-effort beach day, the Royal Beach Club or a Paradise Island beach pass keeps it simple and close.
